# CricVoice Live 🎙️  
### **The Interactive Multilingual Cricket Commentary Sandbox**

**CricVoice Live** is an entertaining, full-featured live sports commentary simulation suite. It bridges the gap between official sports broadcasting and the diverse, high-energy, local flavors of cricket played across India's micro-regions. It empowers fans, creators, and enthusiasts to instantly script, synthesize, and voice custom cricket match updates across **22 official Indian languages**, **20+ authentic regional dialects**, and **10+ expressive entertainment personas**—complete with simulated voice mixing and real-time synthesized stadium acoustic soundboards.

---

## 🎯 Purpose & Vision

In India, cricket is not just a sport; it is an emotion celebrated across hundreds of cultures, languages, and street styles. However, official sports broadcasting is often locked to standard formal commentary registers.  

**CricVoice Live** was built to:
1. **Celebrate Cultural Diversity:** Highlight the rich linguistic spectrum of India, from pure classical languages like Sanskrit to energetic street dialects like Bhojpuri, Haryanvi, Mumbai Tapori, and Bengaluru Techie.
2. **Inject Entertainment & Humor:** Offer unique commentary perspectives, allowing users to experience match updates through the eyes of a hyper-analytical physicist, a dramatic Bollywood director, an urban meme lord, a passionate cup-of-tea street expert, or a nostalgic cricket historian.
3. **Pioneer Interactive Audio Synthesis:** Provide direct access to programmatically generated local crowd sound effects and humanized text-to-speech layers inside the browser, transforming silent text reading into an immersive on-air press-box experience.

---

## 🛠️ The Tech Stack

The application is engineered with a modern, high-performance, and entirely client-centric full-stack architecture:

- **Frontend Core:** **React 19** (Vite-centric, functional components with hooks for highly responsive state changes and audio queue handling).
- **Linguistic Styling & Typographic Identity:** **Tailwind CSS v4** paired with **Inter** for clean, legible UI control flows and **JetBrains Mono** for developer-grade telemetry and statistics readouts.
- **Strong Typing:** **TypeScript** to govern structured language, dialect, persona, and queue states.
- **Generative AI Engine:** **Google Gemini 3.5 Flash** (facilitated server-side via the official `@google/genai` integration) to interpret raw cricket match sentences, analyze stats, and translate them into culturally-faithful, humor-laden commentary scripts.
- **Programmatic Audio Acoustic Synthesizer:** **Web Audio API** triggers programmatically modeled acoustic waves directly in the browser. It synthesizes complex audio without large media assets:
  - *Classic IPL Brass Trumpet Riff* (modeled using sawtooth and detuned triangle oscillators, multi-stage volume envelopes, and bandpass megaphonic filters).
  - *Stadium Crowd Cheers & Roars* (modeled with white noise algorithms, high-pass filtration, and exponential gain curves).
  - *Sfx Soundboard* containing whistlers, rhythmic dhols/drums, and cricket out-horn signals.
- **Speech Engine:** **Web Speech Synthesis API** with dynamic pitch, volume, speaker rates, multi-voice mode emulations, and custom-injected local **Desi Filler Word/Phrase Algorithms** (to insert speech realism e.g., *“Arey bhai!”*, *“Khayiye kasam!”*, *“Sun toh sahi!”*).
- **Interactive Geographics:** Interactive visual representation map of Indian state clusters linked directly to regional linguistic presets.

## 📖 How to Use CricVoice Live (Step-by-Step)

Follow these steps to customize, generate, and broadcast your very own cricket show:

### **Step 1: Set Up Your Match Event**
1. Navigate to the **On-Air Setup** tab.
2. In the text area, type in any custom raw cricket ball description (e.g., *\"Virat Kohli walks down the pitch and lofts the ball straight into the sightscreen for a classy boundary.\"*).
3. Alternatively, click on any of the **Quick Historic Presets** at the top of the desk (e.g., *Dhoni's Historic WC Final Six*, *Kohli's MCG Miracle Six*, *Bumrah's Devastating Yorker*, or a *Chaotic Street Runout*) to load immediate match action.

### **Step 2: Choose Your Language & Dialect Configuration**
1. Scroll down the panel to configure your translation parameters.
2. **Primary Language:** Select from **22 official Indian languages** (Hindi, Bengali, Telugu, Tamil, Sanskrit, Maithili, etc.). You can search, filter by popularity, or bookmark favorites for quick toggle switches.
3. **Hybrid Mode:** (Optional) Enable modern urban spoken mixes like **Hinglish**, **Tanglish**, **Benglish**, or **Punjlish** to blend your selected language seamlessly with colloquial English.
4. **Regional Dialect:** (Optional) Inject deep regional accents into your broadcast by matching your chosen language to an authentic dialect (e.g., *Bhojpuri*, *Haryanvi*, *Braj Bhasha*, *UP Adda Style*, *Mumbai Tapori*, *Mewari*, etc.).

### **Step 3: Overlay an Entertainment Persona**
1. Select. your **On-Air Commentator Persona** to style the delivery format:
   - **Guru Navjot Sidhu:** FRANTIC energy, retro idioms, high-decibel metaphors.
   - **Dr. Stat-O-Man:** Treatment of cricket as formal physics (trajectories, kinetic forces).
   - **Sanjay the Meme Lord:** Deadpan dry humor, modern gamer terms, and internet slangs.
   - **Chintu Gully Legend:** Relatable street-cricket rules (*\"one tap out!\"*, *\"break-glass-and-run-away!\"*).
   - **Karan Johar Style:** Extremely dramatic, violin background vibes, high slow-motion theatrics.
   - **Alysha Glam (Gen-Z Influencer):** Modern lifestyle vlogger styled with *\"slay\"*, *\"main character energy\"*, and *\"literally crying\"*.

### **Step 4: Generate Your Commentary Script**
1. Review your configurations and click the green **Generate Commentary Script** button.
2. The Gemini server-side agent will execute your settings combined with the sports prompt, rendering a beautifully customized script instantly.

### **Step 5: Master and Play the Commentary Vocal**
1. Click the **Voice Mixing & FX** tab.
2. Select your preferred vocal speaker character.
3. Customize vocal elements to create your perfect host:
   - Elevate or decrease the **Vocal Pitch**.
   - Speed up or slow down the **Speech Rate** to match fast-paced over endings.
   - Fine-tune system **Volume**.
4. **Desi Filler Word Controller:** Enable the **Humanizer Algorithm** and slide the **Desi Filler Frequency** bar. This inserts natural humorous expressions dynamically into the speech playback (such as *“Baap re baap!”*, *“O teri!”*, *“Maan gaye guru!”*).
5. Click **Speak Commentary** to let the synthesized computer read your generated masterpiece out loud.

### **Step 6: Ignite Stadium FX & Explore the Interactive Map**
1. Navigate to the **Map & Sounds** tab.
2. Use the **Stadium Soundboard** buttons to ring programmatically generated acoustic sounds during active speech (e.g., hit the *IPL Trumpet* after a boundary or activate *Crowd Roar*).
3. Look at the **Interactive Map of India**: Click on different states (e.g. Punjab, Gujarat, Maharashtra, Karnataka) to instantly see fun regional cricket trivia, learn state capitals, and quickly load their respective localized language and dialect parameters directly back into your control deck.
4. **Continuous Play Queuer:** Load several commentary events in sequence to run a rolling scoreboard broadcast simulation.
